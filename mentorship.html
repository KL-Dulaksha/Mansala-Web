Mentorship Programme

Supporting O/L students through personalised 1:5 mentorship designed to build academic confidence and examination success in Science & Mathematics.

About the Mentorship Programme

The මංසල Manasala mentorship programme connects enthusiastic university student volunteers from the University of Ruhuna with O/L students who are preparing for their Science and Mathematics examinations. By creating supportive small-group learning relationships, we aim to give every student access to guidance that goes beyond the traditional classroom environment. Mentors share not only academic knowledge but also study strategies, exam tips, and the motivation that comes from knowing someone is invested in your success.

Each mentor is a current undergraduate who has excelled in the very subjects they now help teach. They understand the challenges O/L students face because they have been through the same journey. Through regular interactions — whether reviewing difficult concepts, working through past papers, or simply offering encouragement — our mentors help bridge the gap between classroom learning and examination readiness, creating a lasting positive impact on students' educational outcomes.

The 1 : 5 Mentorship Model

Each university mentor is paired with a small group of five O/L students for focused, personalised guidance.

Pedagogical Framework

Three-Stage Mentorship Journey

A structured progression taking students from diagnostic assessment to examination mastery.

Stage 01 • Assessment & Cohort

Diagnostic & Group Formation

Every mentee undergoes a gentle, non-judgmental diagnostic evaluation to identify specific learning hurdles in Science and Mathematics. Mentors establish a dedicated 1:5 study circle with agreed weekly revision targets.

Key Deliverable: Personalised syllabus checklist and baseline competency profile.
Stage 02 • Active Learning

Weekly Problem Dissection & Doubt Clearing

Small-group sessions focus on breaking down difficult past paper questions, practicing complex algebraic formulations, understanding chemical equation balancing, and clarifying textbook ambiguities with university mentors.

Key Deliverable: Weekly worked problem dossiers and doubt resolution logs.
Stage 03 • Examination Mastery

Technique, Timing & Mental Readiness

Mentors coach candidates on examination strategy — navigating the structured essay sections, optimal time allocation per question, accurate scientific drawing, and handling examination anxiety with self-assurance.

Key Deliverable: Timed mock exam simulation and final mark optimization strategy.
