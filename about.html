About මංසල – Mansala

Institutional background, humanitarian outreach vision, academic mission, and volunteer fieldwork framework of the flagship student movement at the University of Ruhuna.

Movement Overview

A Dual Movement of Humanitarian Relief & Academic Empowerment

මංසල – Mansala is a flagship humanitarian and educational outreach movement organised and spearheaded by the Students' Union of the Faculty of Science, University of Ruhuna. Born from a profound sense of social accountability and empathy, Mansala channels the collective energy, intellectual capability, and volunteer spirit of university undergraduates to serve both vulnerable community families and underprivileged rural schools across Sri Lanka.

Operating through two complementary wings — the Humanitarian & Outreach Wing and the Academic Empowerment Wing — Mansala provides rapid, frontline assistance during regional emergencies (such as monsoon floods and natural disasters), delivers direct infrastructure and laboratory apparatus aid to under-resourced schools, and maintains a completely free national study portal for G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) Science and Mathematics candidates.

Every donation, relief package, seminar session, and revision dossier is delivered transparently and entirely free of charge. By connecting higher education directly with grassroots communities, Mansala proves that student-led solidarity can bridge systemic educational gaps and uplift vulnerable lives in times of urgent need.

The Context

Background & Purpose

Why Mansala expanded beyond traditional academic boundaries

Sri Lanka's rural educational landscape faces profound systemic disparities. Students in underserved rural districts often lack access to well-equipped science laboratories, updated library collections, and qualified subject teachers. Consequently, pass rates in foundational subjects such as G.C.E. Ordinary Level Science and Mathematics remain significantly depressed compared to urban centres.

Compounding this educational divide, recurring seasonal natural disasters — particularly monsoon-triggered floods and landslides in the Southern Province and surrounding districts — routinely inundate rural classrooms, destroy school libraries, and displace vulnerable farming and daily-wage families. Under such extreme strain, children's education is often the first casualty.

Recognising that academic excellence cannot flourish in isolation from social welfare, the Faculty of Science Students' Union broadened Mansala from an O/L revision workshop into a comprehensive dual-wing intervention model: combining emergency flood relief and tangible school infrastructure aid with high-impact, free academic resources and mentorship.

Strategic Roadmap

Key Strategic Objectives

Six foundational commitments driving Mansala's humanitarian and educational impact.

Emergency Disaster & Flood Relief

Rapidly deploy volunteer taskforces to flood- and landslide-affected regions to conduct school cleanups, restore sanitation, and deliver dry food rations to vulnerable families.

Rural School Aid & Science Labs

Directly equip under-resourced rural schools with science laboratory apparatus, glassware, chemicals, library books, and essential student stationery kits.

Free Curated Study Dossiers

Author and publish comprehensive, syllabus-aligned revision dossiers, model examination papers, and detailed marking schemes with 100% free digital access.

Cultivate Civic Leadership

Nurture a generation of university undergraduates with frontline empathy, crisis-response leadership, pedagogical skills, and deep civic commitment.

Community Stakeholders

Intended Beneficiaries

Whom the Mansala movement serves and empowers across Sri Lanka.

O/L Students & Rural Candidates

Secondary school students in underprivileged and rural districts preparing for the G.C.E. O/L examination. They receive free master study dossiers, interactive diagnostic quizzes, regional seminar training, and 1:5 mentorship to achieve academic distinction.

Disaster-Affected Families & Communities

Vulnerable families hit by natural calamities such as seasonal floods and landslides. Mansala deploys volunteer taskforces to deliver essential food dry rations, clean water kits, and emergency recovery assistance during acute crises.

Under-Resourced Schools & Regional Teachers

Rural primary and secondary schools facing acute infrastructure and lab deficits. Mansala donates science laboratory equipment, restores library bookshelves, provides classroom stationery packs, and supplies teachers with quality curriculum revision materials.

Undergraduate Volunteers & Emerging Leaders

University of Ruhuna undergraduates who dedicate their talent and energy. Through real-world fieldwork, logistics management, teaching, and crisis relief, they cultivate unmatched leadership, civic empathy, and professional maturity.

Operational Architecture

Two Core Wings of Mansala

Our dual pillars combining frontline humanitarian aid with 100% free national academic empowerment.

Disaster Relief • School Aid ● මානුෂීය සත්කාරය

Humanitarian & Outreach Wing

Direct undergraduate-led fieldwork delivering emergency crisis relief and restoring educational infrastructure across rural Sri Lanka:

  • Emergency Flood & Disaster Cleanup: Hands-on volunteer taskforces assisting flooded schools and homes with mud clearance and sanitary restorations.
  • Dry Ration & Family Care Packages: Essential food hampers and emergency welfare kits for displaced and flood-affected households.
  • Science Lab Apparatus Donations: Donating essential glassware, basic equipment, and chemical sets to schools with neglected laboratories.
  • School Library & Stationery Kits: Providing textbooks, reading material, and full stationery packages to underprivileged children.
  • 100% Transparent Audits: Publicly published verified transfer slips, project completion recaps, and exact expenditure accounting.

Academic Empowerment Wing

Curated learning resources, topic-wise practice quizzes, and structured undergraduate mentorship designed to help every student achieve exam distinction:

  • Curated Study Dossiers: Chapter-by-chapter revision notes, term papers, and step-by-step model marking schemes for Science and Mathematics.
  • Regional In-Person Seminars: Full-day academic workshops with exam question paper walkthroughs conducted directly in rural schools.
  • Interactive Diagnostic Quizzes: Instant self-assessment quizzes with real-time scoring, timers, and comprehensive explanatory answers.
  • 1:5 Undergraduate Mentorship: Small cohort mentoring connecting Ruhuna Science undergraduates with students requiring personalized guidance.
  • 100% Free & Open Access: Zero registration fees, open PDF downloads, and unrestricted access for all teachers, parents, and students.

Our Driving Force

University Student Volunteers

The heart, hands, and intellect driving the Mansala movement.

At the core of Mansala are the undergraduate volunteers from the University of Ruhuna. These dedicated students — across disciplines of Physical Science, Biological Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science — unite under the Faculty of Science Students' Union to dedicate their time, knowledge, and physical labour to transform lives.

Field Relief Volunteers

Lead on-the-ground emergency flood cleanup operations, mud clearance, dry ration packaging, and family welfare distributions in affected regions.

School Aid & Lab Curators

Coordinate school infrastructure assessments, procure laboratory apparatus and chemicals, and organise book collections for rural school libraries.

Academic Content Authors

Research and author curriculum-aligned master revision dossiers, step-by-step marking schemes, and regional seminar test papers for O/L Science and Mathematics.

Undergraduate Mentors

Conduct structured 1:5 small-group mentoring sessions, resolve challenging questions, and provide empathetic academic coaching to help students excel.

Quiz & Assessment Designers

Design interactive topic-wise multiple choice and short-answer diagnostic assessments with immediate scoring and full solution breakdowns.

Logistics & Platform Engineers

Manage the open-access Study Hub, deploy regional seminar audiovisual setups, and ensure transparent public tracking of community initiatives.
