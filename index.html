About the Programme

Community-Driven Educational Outreach

මංසල is a community-driven educational outreach initiative organised by the Students' Union of the Faculty of Science, University of Ruhuna. The programme aims to make O/L Science and Mathematics education more accessible through free learning resources, interactive assessments, and a structured mentorship programme.

Cohort Guidance Model

The 1 : 5 Mentorship Programme

Personalised academic guidance connecting university undergraduate mentors with small cohorts of five O/L candidates.

What Mentors Provide

  • Subject-specific guidance targeting difficult concepts in O/L Science and Mathematics.
  • Structured weekly revision sessions and regular progress review check-ins.
  • Step-by-step past paper discussions, marking scheme breakdowns, and model answers.
  • Personalised study schedules and proven time-management techniques for exam day.

Direct Benefits for Students

  • 100% Free: No charges, fees, or hidden conditions for any participant.
  • Dedicated Guidance: The structured 1:5 ratio ensures every student receives dedicated academic guidance.
  • Relatable Mentors: Guided by undergraduate achievers who recently cleared O/Ls.
  • Confidence & Mastery: Overcome subject apprehension through structured practice.
