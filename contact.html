Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the programme, resources, and participation.

What is මංසල – Mansala?

මංසල – Mansala is an educational outreach programme organised by the Students' Union of the Faculty of Science of the University of Ruhuna. It is designed to support Ordinary Level (O/L) students in Sri Lanka by providing free learning resources, practice quizzes, and mentorship in Science and Mathematics. The programme is run entirely by volunteer university students committed to helping the next generation succeed.

Who can use the learning resources?

The learning resources on this platform are created for students preparing for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) examination, particularly in Science and Mathematics. However, anyone interested in learning these subjects is welcome to access the materials. All resources are provided free of charge.

How do I access practice quizzes?

You can access all available practice quizzes by visiting the Quizzes page. Quizzes are organised by subject and topic, and each quiz indicates its difficulty level and estimated completion time. Quizzes are self-paced, completely free, and provide instant results along with step-by-step worked answers.

Is there a cost to participate in Manasala?

No. මංසල – Mansala is a completely free programme. All learning resources, quizzes, mentorship sessions, and activities are offered at no cost to students. The programme is funded and supported by the Students' Union of the Faculty of Science of the University of Ruhuna and its volunteer network.

How can I become a volunteer mentor?

If you are a current student at the University of Ruhuna and would like to volunteer as a mentor, you can express your interest through the Mentorship page or contact the Programme Coordinator using the details provided above. Mentors are matched with small groups of O/L students to provide academic guidance and support.

How do I report an issue with the website?

If you encounter a technical issue, broken link, or any problem with the website, please use the Help Desk enquiry form above and select "Technical Issue" as the category. Provide as much detail as possible, including what page you were on and what went wrong. Our team will review and address the issue promptly.
