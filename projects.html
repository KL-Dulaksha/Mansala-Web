TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN — OFFICIAL INSTITUTIONAL ENDORSEMENT

This is to formally certify that "මංසල – Mansala" is an approved humanitarian outreach, community relief, and educational initiative organised by the undergraduate students and the Science Faculty Students' Union of the University of Ruhuna.

The movement operates under the academic guidance of faculty members and the financial supervision of the Senior Treasurer. All contributions collected through this official portal are strictly allocated for:

  • Emergency disaster & flood relief, classroom restoration, and dry ration distribution.
  • Procuring essential science lab apparatus, student microscopes, and library reference books for underprivileged rural schools.
  • Printing syllabus-aligned model exam workbooks and volunteer undergraduate travel logistics.

All bank accounts specified on this domain (BOC Account No. 00084729104) are registered under the official Faculty of Science Students' Union. Complete financial statements are subjected to annual university audit procedures.
